# 终止权 / The Right to Terminate

Public reading edition of a science-fiction story, with the expanded Chinese novel (25 chapters and epilogue), a 120-page Chinese comic, and the original 79-page English comic.

Read: https://openspyusa.github.io/

The author devised the story and directed revisions. Generative AI assisted with research, prose drafting, translation and comic imagery. Comic dialogue and labels were typeset with actual fonts.

Future actions, offices, dialogue and election outcomes involving real people and companies are fictional. This is fiction. The repository contains only public reading pages and publication assets. Private drafts, editorial records, correspondence and other workspace material are not included.
